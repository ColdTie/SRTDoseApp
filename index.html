How the dose looks

Teaching model only, not for treatment planning. Geometry follows the Xstrahl RADiant Aura (6 cm source distance, 10 to 40 mm acrylic applicators). Depth dose from 0 to 4 mm and beam on times come from the clinic’s SRT treatment time table. Deeper dose, the beam edge and out of field scatter are estimates based on published low kV measurements. Tissue layers use average adult forearm values: skin 1.4 mm, fat 6 mm.
